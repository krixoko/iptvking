2. Overview of processing

No IPTV KING user account is required. The library and settings are managed on the device; there is no proprietary IPTV KING server synchronizing them. This does not mean that no data leaves the device: source requests, Google services, selected external players, exports and website visits can involve transfers.

The app has no proprietary analytics feature tracking your library use. In Free, Google and the advertising partners listed in the consent dialog may process data for advertising, measurement and, where permitted, personalization.

3. Local storage, backups and recordings

Playlists including credentials, settings, favorites, history, reminders and recording schedules are managed in private app storage. Library and settings data are encrypted with AES-GCM using a device-bound Android Keystore key. This is not a guarantee of encryption for every file or network transfer. Media recordings are stored as playable files and are not protected by this library encryption.

An exported library backup contains the library and settings, including stored credentials, but no recordings. It is encrypted with your password (PBKDF2, 210,000 iterations, AES-GCM). Recordings can be exported separately. The Android file picker may offer local or cloud destinations; the chosen service receives the exported file. Backup encryption depends on a strong password kept secret.

Automatic Android app backup and device transfer are disabled. This does not cover manual exports. Uninstalling removes private app data, not previously exported or shared copies.

4. Sources, logos, external players and Cast

Importing, refreshing, playback and recording connect to your sources and the media, guide, key and logo addresses they reference. Recipients receive data such as IP address, request time, requested address and required credentials. Redirects and third-party addresses inside playlists can involve additional recipients. Automatic updates, when enabled, can make background requests.

External playback passes the playback URL to the selected app. Cast passes the media address and playback metadata to the selected receiver. Addresses can contain credentials. Both editions include the Google Cast framework, which may initialize before an active casting session; inactive casting does not mean every Google component is inactive. See Google’s privacy policy.

HTTP is unencrypted: HTTP traffic, including credentials and content, may be read in transit. Prefer HTTPS. Local encryption does not protect network traffic. Our app code does not pass playlist URLs or credentials to AdMob as advertising parameters.

5. Advertising and consent in Free

Free uses Google AdMob for menu banners and occasional full-screen ads before playback. Processing may include IP address, approximate region, device/advertising identifiers, diagnostic data and ad interactions. Depending on consent and configuration, Google and selected advertising partners may use data for personalization, measurement and fraud prevention. The UMP dialog explains partners and purposes. The app provider retains its responsibilities for integrating these services.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) refreshes consent information at app launch and loads a form when needed. This requires a connection to Google even if you subsequently reject advertising. Where consent is required, the app permits advertising only when UMP allows ad requests and consent to storing/accessing device information (TCF purpose 1) is present. Other consent signals govern permitted ad processing. Outside regions requiring consent, UMP may report that consent is not required.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, choosing “Do not consent” prevents the app from loading ads. Change your decision through the information icon in the library header under Ad privacy, without a parental PIN. The existing entry under More also remains available. Opening these options removes banners and discards preloaded full-screen ads. Withdrawal applies prospectively; it does not reverse past processing or undo an SDK’s earlier initialization. Rejecting ads does not block media features.

For Google’s processing, recipients and choices, see information about partner services, Google’s privacy policy and the partner details in the consent dialog.

6. Pro and distribution through Google Play

Pro includes neither AdMob nor UMP and displays no ads. It still connects to sources and includes the shared Cast framework. Ad-free does not mean no data transfers.

The listed Pro features are included in this edition without additional in-app activation. Content access is not included. Prices, contract terms and availability are specified in the relevant store offer. When obtaining the app through Google Play, Google processes necessary account, installation and, where applicable, payment data under its terms. The app code contains no Google Play Billing SDK. These notices do not exclude statutory consumer rights.

7. Permissions and limitations

Internet access serves sources and integrated services. Foreground services and temporary wake locks support recordings. Notifications and alarms support recording status, reminders and scheduling. Boot events support rescheduling and optional Pro startup. The “Display over other apps” permission allows automatic Pro startup under Android rules; ordinary playback does not require it. Android and manufacturer restrictions may delay or prevent startup or recordings.

The app declares no permission for camera, microphone, contacts or precise device location. IP addresses can still reveal an approximate region. File access includes the files and destinations you select in system dialogs for import, restoration or export.
