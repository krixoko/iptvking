2. Overview

IPTV KING is a player for IPTV content. The app has no user account, no sign-up and no login. No profiles are created, and no usage data is sent to the app's provider.

The content you watch comes from sources you add yourself — M3U playlists, Xtream credentials, EPG files and channel logos. Those sources are not operated by the app's provider.

3. Data that stays on your device

Your playlists, settings, favourites, playback history, reminders, parental PIN and recordings are stored exclusively in the app's private directory on your device.

  • Library and settings are stored encrypted in library.enc and preferences.enc.
  • Encryption uses AES-GCM; the key is generated in the device's Android Keystore and never leaves the device.
  • Recordings are written as files on the device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes this data.

A backup you export yourself contains your library and settings and is encrypted with a password you choose (PBKDF2 with 210,000 iterations, then AES-GCM). Without that password the file cannot be restored — not by the app's provider either. Where you store or share the file is your decision; the app's provider does not receive it.

4. Connections to your providers

When playing content or refreshing channel lists and programme data, the app connects directly to the addresses you entered. Those providers therefore receive technically necessary information such as your IP address and the time of the request. What they store is governed by their own terms, over which the app's provider has no influence.

Note on transport: The app also accepts unencrypted http:// addresses, because many IPTV providers do not offer HTTPS. With such addresses the transmitted data is not encrypted in transit and can be read on the network. Local encryption on the device does not change this. Use https:// addresses where possible.

Playlist addresses and credentials are not passed on as advertising parameters.

5. Advertising — IPTV KING Free only

IPTV KING Free shows advertising via Google AdMob: a banner in the menu areas and occasionally a full-screen ad before playback starts. For this, Google may process your device's advertising ID along with technical details such as device type, country and approximate location.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

On first launch, a consent dialog from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown. The advertising SDK is technically only started once consent has been given. If you decline, no advertising is loaded and the app remains fully usable.

You can change or withdraw your decision at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy.

Google is the controller for the processing carried out by AdMob. Details: Google – How Google uses information from partner sites.

6. IPTV KING Pro

IPTV KING Pro contains no advertising libraries. Neither AdMob nor UMP is bundled, no advertising ID is requested and no consent dialog is shown.

7. Google Cast

When you start casting, the app looks for receivers on your local network and passes the playback address to the selected device, using Google's Cast framework. Without an active cast session this feature is not used.

8. Permissions

  • Internet — fetching channels, programme data and logos.
  • Foreground service — recordings continue while the app is in the background.
  • Notifications — recording status and programme reminders.
  • Exact alarms — scheduled recordings and reminders start on time.
  • Start on boot — scheduled recordings survive a device restart.
  • Wake lock — prevents interruptions during a recording.
  • Display over other apps — optional overlay playback; only effective if you allow it in system settings.

The app requests no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or your personal files.

9. No cloud backup

Android auto backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app (allowBackup="false"). Your app data is therefore not uploaded to a Google account and not carried over automatically to a new device.

10. Legal bases

Where the app's provider processes anything at all, the following applies:

  • Advertising and access to the advertising ID (Free only) — your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Without consent the advertising SDK is not started.
  • Connecting to your own sources — performance of the contract of use under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Connections are made only to addresses you enter yourself.
  • Local storage on the device — this data does not leave your device and is not processed by the app's provider.

Transfers to third countries: If you consent to advertising, Google may also process data outside the European Union, in particular in the United States. Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses under Art. 46 GDPR.

11. Retention

Locally stored data remains on the device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data via system settings, or uninstall the app. No data is retained by the app's provider. How long Google retains advertising data is governed by Google's own terms.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children. A PIN-protected parental control feature is available and works locally on the device.

13. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

The app's provider operates no user account and stores no information by which a request could be attributed to a particular person; Art. 11 GDPR applies accordingly. Requests concerning processing within AdMob are best addressed directly to Google. Locally stored data you erase yourself by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. You withdraw your advertising consent under Settings → Ad privacy.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
